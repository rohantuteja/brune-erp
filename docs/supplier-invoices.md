# Supplier invoices and other bills: reading the PDFs for Tally

**Goal.** When the ERP pushes to Tally it must create the vendor's ledger if it is missing and post the purchase voucher or the expense voucher (and every advance paid before the invoice came) without anyone typing an invoice. The same upload page takes **every** vendor invoice, not only fabric: Meta, Velocity, Easebuzz, GoKwik and any other. Nothing here posts to Tally yet. The plan was agreed on 15 Oct 2026; it is built in four slices, each reviewed before it goes live.

| Slice | What | State |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The reader (`_shared/invoice-reader.ts`) and its tests: pure code, no database, no network | merged (PR #28) |
| 2 | **Analytics → Bills**: upload any vendor's invoice PDF, the bill store, a private bucket for the originals, a booking profile per vendor, confirm-once then automatic | built, see below; applies to live data only on the owner's go-ahead |
| 3 | Bank matching: advances waiting for an invoice (flag after 15 days) and unpaid invoices; the payee → supplier link | not started |
| 4 | The "Tally-ready" purchase voucher preview and the item map | not started |

## Decisions (owner, 14-15 Oct 2026)
- **Every vendor invoice goes through the same page.** A vendor has a booking profile, set once at its first invoice and remembered: **goods** (fabric: a `Purchase UP` voucher with stock items), **expense** (the accountant's `GST EXPENSES` voucher type: Dr an expense ledger + Input GST, Cr the vendor; Meta → Marketing Expenses, Velocity → Shipping Expenses @ 18 %, GoKwik's COD and PP COD invoices → Payment Gateway Expenses, Easebuzz → tie-out only, Goexcelsior and Google → Software Expenses @ 18 %, Airtel → Telephone and Internet Expenses GST), **tie-out only** (never posted: Easebuzz) or **foreign / no GST** (Shopify, ChatGPT, Claude: no GSTIN; not built yet). Meta's 2 % TDS from October and Velocity's freight provision are for the Tally slice and the accountant.
- PDFs arrive only through the upload page (no mailbox). The original PDF is stored for records.
- **No paid service and no AI call**: the reader is rules plus arithmetic. (The Anthropic API was considered and declined: the owner does not want a usage bill.)
- No manual invoice form. The first invoice from each supplier is confirmed once; later ones are accepted automatically when every check passes, and anything doubtful is held.
- Invoices dated before 1 Oct 2026 are opening bills: used for matching, never exported.
- The ERP inventory module (physical fabric, for production) is NOT matched to invoice lines. The purchase voucher follows the invoice as printed; stock in Tally is for value. Kilos go to `KNITTED FABRIC` (KGS), metres to `Shirting Fabric` (MTR), with the HSN of each invoice line. Whether closing stock is a month-end value entry from the ERP shelf value is the accountant's call.
- Tally names, taken from the accountant's own sample `Purchase UP` voucher and trial balance: voucher type `Purchase UP`, the voucher number and reference are the supplier's invoice number, ledger `Purchase Raw Material`, GST on `IGST Input  UP` (two spaces; `CGST Input UP` + `SGST Input UP` for a supplier in Uttar Pradesh), rounding on `ROUND OFF`, godown `Main Location`, batch `Primary Batch`, supplier ledger under its trade name with GSTIN, state and PAN. Open question for the accountant: does he park purchase GST on `INPUT NOT CLAIMED FOR INV NOT IN 2A` until the invoice shows in GSTR-2B?

## Slice 2: Analytics → Bills
**Page** (`src/pages/BillsSection.jsx`, admin only, `/analytics/bills`): drop one or more PDFs (up to 5 MB each); each is read and lands as *Needs review* or *Accepted*. A bill opens beside the PDF of its original, with the eight checks, fields that can be corrected, the vendor and how it is booked, and Confirm / Void. A *Vendors* section lists each vendor's booking and Tally ledger.

**Function** (`bills-import`, admin only): `upload` (hash, duplicate check, read, vendor match, store the original in the private `vendor-bills` bucket, one result per invoice found), `confirm`, `vendor_save`, `void`, `file_url` (a link to the original that expires in five minutes). The rules are in `_shared/bills.ts` (pure, tested).

**Tables** (migration 034): `vendors` (name, its own Tally ledger, GSTIN, how it is booked, `auto_accept`), seven vendors already set up from the accountant's September vouchers and the owner's corrections (Meta, Velocity, Easebuzz, GoKwik, Goexcelsior, Google, Airtel); `vendor_invoices` gains the vendor, goods/service, `status` (`accepted` / `review` / `void`), `opening`, the checks, the original (`file_path`, `file_hash`, `file_name`), who confirmed it and what they edited, why a failed check was accepted, why it was voided; `app_settings.company_gstin`.

**Behaviour**
- **Booking per vendor**: `expense` (a GST EXPENSES voucher: Dr the expense ledger + input GST, Cr the vendor), `purchase` (a Purchase UP voucher), `tie_out` (never posted, only compared with something else: Easebuzz). The expense ledger is a bucket (a Tally ledger name). A vendor is found by GSTIN; one with no GSTIN yet (Goexcelsior) by name, and learns its GSTIN at its first confirmed bill.
- **Accepted by itself** only when the invoice read cleanly, every check passed, the vendor is complete and **one bill of it was confirmed before**. Anything else is *Needs review*, with the reasons. A goods invoice from an expense vendor (or a service invoice from a purchase vendor) is held.
- **Confirming**: choose or set up the vendor; correct the invoice number, date and amounts if needed (taxable + GST = total within ₹1 is enforced); a failed check blocks the bill unless it is accepted anyway with a reason of at least five characters, which is kept. Confirming a clean bill turns the vendor automatic.
- **A bill already in the system without a PDF** (the Meta and Velocity invoices, typed in before there was an upload) takes the PDF when it is uploaded; the figures stored are compared with the PDF and any difference is shown. The same file twice, or the same invoice twice, changes nothing.
- **Void**, never delete. A voided bill no longer proves a bank payment (`finance-import` v7 ignores `status = 'void'`). A Velocity bill is voided on the Velocity wallet page, which also reads it.
- **Opening bills** (dated before `accounting_start_date`) are kept and matched, never exported.
- A PDF with no text (a scan) or that is not an invoice is refused with the reason; nothing is stored.
- pdf.js takes over the buffer it is given: the function reads a copy, because the original is stored from the same bytes (a test caught this: the stored file was empty).

**Rollout order** (each step needs the owner's go-ahead): apply migration 034; deploy `bills-import` (new); deploy `finance-import` v7 (needs the `status` column); merge the page. Done 15 Oct 2026. The Tally ledgers change (migration 035) rolls out the same way: apply 035, deploy `bills-import` v2, merge the page.

**Tally's own ledgers (migration 035, 15 Oct 2026).** The page never asks for a ledger name to be typed. `tally_ledgers` holds the 198 ledgers of the owner's trial balance of 1-Apr-26 to 30-Sep-26 (`TrialBal.xlsx`, ledgers only, no groups) with the group above each, plus the agreed renames (`Velocity Prepaid Recharge Account` for `Shipfast-Prepaid Recharge Account`, `COD receivable (Velocity)`, `Director Salary Payable- Adrija Halder`) and the six ledgers still to create (`tally-changes-needed.xlsx`): the ERP uses the new names, and the page says "Tally still calls it ..." until the accountant renames them. A vendor's own ledger is picked from the party ledgers (Sundry Creditors and Sundry Debtors, 35), its expense ledger from the 42 Direct / Indirect Expense and Purchase Accounts ledgers, grouped as in Tally. A new vendor is offered the ledger Tally already has for the supplier: an exact name, or the one party ledger that is the same name spelled differently (company suffixes and brackets ignored; a different firm sharing a word, such as Mittal Textile and Mittal Hosiery, is only listed as "did you mean"). A name Tally does not have needs the box "not in my Tally yet: the export creates it" (`vendors.ledger_new`), because a typo would make Tally create a second ledger. `bills-import` enforces the same rules (`LedgerBook` in `_shared/bills.ts`). `vendors.expense_ledger` now references `tally_ledgers`, not the bank-matching buckets. A newer trial balance replaces the list; until then a ledger created later is added as "new". Finding on loading it: `FINANCE COST` and `Purchase Accounts` are groups in Tally, not ledgers (the bank buckets of those names are markers, not postings).

**Easebuzz is tie-out only (owner's final decision, 15 Oct 2026).** The order ledger books its fee and the GST on it at each GoKwik payout (Dr bank net, Dr Payment Gateway Expenses, Dr IGST Input, Cr Easebuzz gross), so the Easebuzz invoice is never posted as well: it is stored with its PDF and has to agree with that total. September agrees to the paisa (₹12,618.85 both ways; the split between fee and GST is ₹0.21 apart). The owner considered posting the invoice like every other vendor's and using the report as the check, then chose to leave the order ledger alone; the change (payout booked net, the invoice clearing the fee left in the Easebuzz account, the per-order "gateway holds nothing" check restated per month) was not made.

## The reader (slice 1)
`readInvoicePdf(items, { companyGstin, today })` takes the text of the PDF with its position (pdf.js through `unpdf`, the library the card-statement reader already uses; **not pypdf, which garbles TallyPrime PDFs**) and returns every invoice in the file.

How it reads, since the layouts differ between billing programs:
- **GSTIN**: a fixed shape with a check digit; the first one that is neither ours nor the transporter's is the supplier's. The state and the PAN come from it.
- **Invoice number and date**: found by their label (`Invoice No`, `Invoice Date`, `Date of Invoice`, `Dated`), the value being the next thing to the right or below.
- **Amounts are solved, not located**: a taxable value, a standard GST rate (0.25, 3, 5, 12, 18, 28 %), a GST amount that is that rate of it (IGST, or two equal halves for CGST + SGST) and a total within a rupee of rounding, wherever they are printed.
- **Line items**: by column. Every header is a column, a number goes to the nearest one, a "Qty Unit" cell is split, and the description is whatever sits between the serial number and the HSN (headers are often centred over a left-aligned description).
- **Supplier name**: the "for <name>" line above the signature.
- **Copies and continuation**: an invoice printed twice in one file (original for the recipient, duplicate for the transporter) is one invoice read once; a page with no supplier or invoice number continues the one before.

Each invoice carries checks: supplier GSTIN check digit, billed to our GSTIN, invoice number, date (not in the future), amounts agree, IGST vs CGST + SGST fits the states, lines add up to the taxable value, quantity × rate = amount for every line. `status` is `ok` when all pass, `review` when something was read but a check failed, `unreadable` when neither a supplier nor an invoice number was found; a PDF with no text at all comes back as `problem: 'no_text'`.

**Services (Meta, Velocity, a gateway fee).** An invoice with a SAC and no HSN is `kind: 'service'`: booked from its totals, so there are no quantities to check. A table of SAC, description and amount is kept as lines (Velocity's); a campaign list or a free-form table (Meta's) is not read as lines. A taxable value that is not printed (Velocity prints two lines and the GST) is the lines added up. A page that says "Page 2 of 2" is the next page of its invoice even though it repeats the header; a page that merely repeats the invoice is a copy.

**Known limits.** One GST rate per invoice (an invoice mixing 5 % and 12 % lines comes back for review); a bill with no GSTIN (a foreign vendor, an electricity or cash bill) comes back `unreadable` for now; a PDF that is only a picture has no text; a layout the reader has not met may be held for review. It was built against 13 real invoices: six fabric (five billing programs, IGST 5 %), and seven service invoices (Meta, Velocity, Easebuzz, four from GoKwik, a Google Workspace invoice and a telecom bill; IGST 18 %, and the telecom bill is the one CGST + SGST invoice, from a supplier in Uttar Pradesh). A fabric supplier in Uttar Pradesh, a bill that is not a GST invoice at all, and an invoice with several rates are the cases not yet seen.

**Kind is decided by the codes**, not the headings: a SAC always starts with 99 and an HSN never does, so an invoice whose codes (in rows that carry an amount) all start with 99 is a service, even when its heading says "HSN" (a telecom bill). **Billed to us** is our GSTIN on the invoice, or (Google's format) our PAN together with our state code, which is the body of the GSTIN and is what the supplier reports in GSTR-2B; the owner confirmed Google's invoices show up there and the accountant books their IGST Input. Our PAN alone, or another PAN, is held for review.

**Fixtures.** `_shared/fixtures/invoices/*.json` are those thirteen invoices as pdf.js text items with every name, GSTIN, address, phone number and bank detail replaced; the layout is untouched. Never add a real invoice to the repo: replace the personal details first.
